AI & Full-Stack Developer · Individual project

Gemini 2.5 FlashFlaskReactSQLite

An AI-assisted agricultural marketplace that turns natural-language seller input into structured, reviewable product listings.

Face Recognition System

Computer Vision Contributor · 7-member team

RetinaFaceArcFaceInsightFaceONNX Runtime

A multi-pipeline face-recognition study; I implemented and evaluated the team’s RetinaFace + ArcFace branch.

  • Implemented the RetinaFace + ArcFace pipeline with InsightFace buffalo_s and ONNX Runtime, including input padding for oversized faces, landmark-based alignment, L2-normalized ArcFace embeddings, cosine-similarity matching, and open-set rejection.
